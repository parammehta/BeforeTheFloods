Introduction

Global warming is one of the biggest problem that the world is facing right now. The atmosphere is being damaged because of the increasing greenhouse gas emissions. Physical impacts of global warming are growing and something needs be done to save our Earth before it suffers from any more damage. Our proposed project is a user-interactive web-based application which would help people understand about weather and pollution in their city and at a global level. We would start from a city of the user’s choice so they can get information about climate change and pollution level in their city which would interest him/her to search at a national level and then at an international level.
