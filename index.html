Introduction

Will we survive before the floods?

Global warming is one of the biggest problems the world faces. The atmosphere is being damaged by rising greenhouse gas emissions, the physical impacts are growing, and something has to be done before the damage compounds further.

This is a user-interactive story about weather, pollution and land use. It starts with a city of your choosing, so you can see the climate and pollution picture where you live, then widens out to the national and global scale.
